The idea

What message do you get from Jesus, if no one else is doing the talking?

The Bible is mostly not Jesus. It's four gospel writers, a handful of apostles, and centuries of translators and commentators — all working around the words he actually spoke.

This page strips all of that away. What's left is only what he said out loud. The red-letter tradition marks these passages in red ink; here they are, in one continuous read, in the order they appear in the New Testament.

Read them without a filter. See what emerges.
